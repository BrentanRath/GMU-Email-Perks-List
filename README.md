# George Mason Email Perk List
This is an unofficial list of everything I have found so far that you can get once you are a student at GMU.
Some of these come with your email, some you can register with your email. (Some are GMU specific, or .edu in general.)

## Microsoft
- [Bookings](https://outlook.office.com/bookings) - Simplify how you schedule and manage appointments both inside and outside your organization.
- [Calendar](https://outlook.office.com/calendar) - Manage and share your schedule.
- [Clipchamp](https://app.clipchamp.com/) - Make and edit videos.
- [Connections](https://viva.cloud.microsoft) - Access personalized tools, news, and resources.
- [Copilot](https://copilot.microsoft.com) - Your AI companion for work and productivity.
- [Engage](https://engage.cloud.microsoft) - Connect with coworkers and classmates, share information, and organize around projects.
- [Excel](https://excel.cloud.microsoft) - Budget, plan, and calculate.
- [Forms](https://forms.cloud.microsoft) - Customize surveys and quizzes, get real-time results.
- [Learning Activities](https://viva.cloud.microsoft) - AI-powered activities that spark better learning.
- [Lists](https://www.office.com/launch/lists) - Create, share, and track data inside lists.
- [Loop](https://loop.cloud.microsoft) - Enabling teams to think, plan, and create together.
- [OneDrive](https://portal.office.com/onedrive) - Safely store files, photos, and more.
- [OneNote](https://www.office.com/launch/onenote) - Create a digital notebook.
- [Org Explorer](https://outlook.office.com/people) - Visualize your entire organization in a single, intuitive and interactive view.
- [Outlook](https://outlook.cloud.microsoft) - Email, schedule, and set tasks.
- [People](https://outlook.office.com/people) - Group, share, and manage contacts.
- [Planner](https://planner.cloud.microsoft) - Create plans, organize and assign tasks, share files, and get progress updates.
- [Power Apps](https://make.powerapps.com) - Build mobile and web apps with the data your organization already uses.
- [Power Pages](https://make.powerpages.microsoft.com) - Craft secure, low-code business websites with ease.
- [PowerPoint](https://powerpoint.cloud.microsoft) - Easily make presentations.
- [Reading Coach](https://readingcoach.microsoft.com/) - AI-powered personalized reading fluency practice.
- [Reflect](https://reflect.microsoft.com/) - A wellbeing app to support connection, expression, and learning.
- [SharePoint](https://www.office.com/launch/sharepoint) - Share and manage content, knowledge, and applications to empower teamwork.
- [Stream](https://stream.office.com) - Share videos of classes, meetings, presentations, and training sessions.
- [Sway](https://sway.cloud.microsoft) - Create interactive reports and presentations.
- [Teams](https://teams.cloud.microsoft) - Meet, share, and chat.
- [To Do](https://to-do.office.com) - List and manage your tasks.
- [Visio](https://m365.cloud.microsoft/launch/visio) - Simplify and communicate complex information visually.
- [Whiteboard](https://whiteboard.office.com) - Ideate and collaborate on a freeform canvas.
- [Word](https://word.cloud.microsoft) - Write, share, and make real-time edits.

- [Github Student Developer Pack](https://education.github.com/pack) - A bundle of $2,000 worth of software, bundles and websites for programmers, and tech nerds.
